About IT Lab

Senior engineering, without the agency overhead

IT Lab was founded by Patrick Ndimande, a software engineer with more than eight years of experience building and modernising business systems in .NET, Angular, Flutter and the cloud.

When you work with us, you speak directly to the person building your product. We plan the work with you, agree how it will be tested, and release in small steps you can see and try. Nothing goes live until it has passed its builds, automated tests and checks against a real test environment.

  • Full ownership, from API to app store
  • AI-assisted delivery with strict quality checks
  • Microsoft Azure and AWS experience
  • Ongoing support after launch

How we work
